Supplements are regulated as food, not as medicine. That means nobody checks
what is in the tub before it reaches you — the manufacturer's word is the only
guarantee, unless an independent lab has looked at it.

## What a certificate of analysis covers

A certificate of analysis (CoA) is a lab report for one specific batch. A useful
one reports:

- **Identity** — the ingredient is what the label says it is.
- **Potency** — how much active compound is actually present.
- **Contaminants** — heavy metals, pesticides, solvent residues.
- **Microbiology** — yeast, mould and bacterial counts.

## What it does not cover

A CoA says nothing about whether the dose is useful, whether the form is
absorbable, or whether the ingredient does anything at all. Those are separate
questions, and a clean lab report is not an answer to them.

## How we handle it

Every batch we ship is tested by an ISO 17025 accredited lab, and the report is
published with the batch number printed on the tub.
